Who I Am

About Me

I'm a detail-oriented Data Analyst based in Burewala, Punjab, currently pursuing a BS in Software Engineering at University of Agriculture Faisalabad. I specialise in turning complex, messy datasets into clear, actionable insights that power smarter decisions.

My stack covers Power BI, Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib), SQL, DAX, Power Query, and Excel. Whether it's HR workforce analytics, real-time weather forecasting, or large-scale data modelling, I find the story inside the numbers and communicate it visually.

I'm driven by a passion for KPI design, data modelling, and building dashboards that are both technically rigorous and visually intuitive for stakeholders at every level.
